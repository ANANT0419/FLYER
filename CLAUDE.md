# Flyer: notes for whoever continues this

Wingsuit gliding game (three.js r128 + Vite). Live at https://flyer-aspiring-yogi.vercel.app; every push to `main` redeploys on Vercel (project `flyer`, team `aspiring-yogi`). See README.md for the file map and controls.

## How the owner works
- **"Make me play and then you observe everything"**: every flight is recorded and sent to Supabase table `public.flight_logs` (project `dxzuwqqbmdmkywhmdfje`; the game may only insert). Start each session by reading the owner's latest flights with the Supabase MCP, e.g. `select id, created_at, meta from flight_logs order by id desc limit 10`, then the `data` of one (`keys`, columnar `cols` at 20 Hz, `events`). Columns: t, s (distance), off (m off the line), lk (lock), ol (on line), pi/ti (player pitch/turn input), np/nt (what the line needed), hy/hp (help added), p/r/yw (attitude), v (speed), y/gy (height above floor/ground), mdt (longest frame ms in the 50 ms), pp/pr (phone raw tilt), fp/fr (phone after prediction+filter), pl (phone live), ms (network). `src/recorder.js`; switch "Send my flights for review" on the start screen (default on).
- Wants to be asked for input on real design choices, then have it built; gives feedback in short bullet rounds after playtesting on laptop and iPhone.
- Controls are the top priority: they must feel as smooth as possible. Visuals come after.
- Keep the in-game issue log working in everything.
- Test before handing over, and say plainly what could not be tested (no GPU, camera or real phone in the cloud sandbox).
- Explain in plain language; the owner is not a programmer.

## State
- Controls: arrow keys, mouse, two arms, touch, phone glider (`src/link.js`, `src/pad.js`, relay in `src/relay.js`), camera with open palm (default), flat hand or any object (`src/hand.js`, `src/objtrack.js`).
- Phone glider: direct WebRTC via public PeerJS, Supabase Realtime relay fallback (project `flyer`, ap-south-1). Owner confirmed it connects; reported lag and that the setup screen's move list never ticked. Fixed: list ticks, tilt sent per sensor reading, screen leads slightly between messages, delay shown on setup screen. Not yet re-tested by the owner.
- Open-palm camera mode is new and untested on a real camera. The flat-hand mode was reported jittery.
- Terrain: cliffs with ledges, rim crags, ridged mountain ranges (replaced the cone mountains). `?view=sky` for an overhead check.
- Message budget: the Supabase free plan allows ~100 Realtime messages/s for the whole project. The owner's first live race hit it ("MessagePerSecondRateLimitReached", 429s in realtime logs) and phone tilt on the relay was dropped. Now: one shared client per page (`src/rt.js`), nothing sent while disconnected (no REST fallback), relay tilt ≤15/s and only on change, race positions 4/s. Check `realtime_logs` with the Supabase MCP if races stutter again; upgrading the plan or moving race traffic peer-to-peer are the next steps.
- Latency (owner: "finish the delay, not just minimise it", then "make it zero practically"): the phone sends each reading with its gyro rate (units and sign auto-fitted against the change in angle, `src/predict.js`) and its own timestamp. The screen syncs clocks from its pings, dates each reading, and draws where the phone is now plus ~50 ms (display + smoothing). Game easing for phone attitude ~30 ms (kP 35; 70 let hand tremor through), camera control stays ~70 ms. Simulated (`node test/predict-sim.mjs`): direct Wi-Fi 106 → 4 ms behind, relay 164 → 48 ms. Two-browser link test (`node test/phone-e2e.cjs`, `DELAY=40` adds network delay; needs `npx peerjs --port 9000 --host 127.0.0.1` and vite on 5173): 58 → 1 ms. Not yet felt on a real phone: watch for overshoot when stopping a fast tilt (lead capped at 18° direct / 25° relay). Round 3 (owner: "all of the game is fucked, the view was following the player"): (1) a comment placed mid-line in `updateCamera` had disabled `camera.lookAt` — the camera stopped turning toward the glider. Fixed, camera follow back to the old ~90 ms, and `test/full-check.cjs` now checks the camera aims at the glider every frame on every course/control/level (it catches that bug). (2) The owner's phone check said `gyroscope learning`: iPhones order the three gyro numbers differently, so the fit never trusted any; prediction ran on noisy angle differences with up to 18° lead = shaking. Now all three gyro numbers are fitted to each tilt (`rateStep`), prediction leads at most 4° until the gyro is learned, and a speed-adaptive "one euro" filter (`euroStep`) removes tremor while still. `node test/phone-shake.mjs` (iPhone-like stream): played version 4 ms / wobble 0.94, now 18 ms / 0.18, no prediction 106 ms / 0.10. A **phone check** (link, network ms, readings/s from the phone, gyroscope on/learning/off, screen fps) shows on the setup screen and goes into the issue log 8 s into a phone flight: ask the owner for that line when they report delay. Ghosts are dead-reckoned (race.js). A red HUD chip shows when the phone is on the relay.
- Sun: owner said too bright (round 4). Sky sun terms, rays and glare sprite halved, bloom 0.34/threshold 0.86, and the grade softens the brightest areas (`post.js`).
- Performance: owner saw fps drops after the terrain work. Far ranges now use per-vertex colours + Lambert (not the per-pixel terrain shader), canyon mesh 81 columns, no crack noise; quality ladder steps down at <50 fps (brushwork, resolution 1.25, 1.0, bloom, 0.8), each step noted in the issue log.
- Phone pairing code is kept in sessionStorage, so switching to the race canyon (a page reload) keeps the phone; the phone retries by itself.
- Racing: Razorback Canyon (`RACE` in game.js, chosen at page load; `?room=CODE` joins a room). Rooms in `src/race.js` on the Supabase project. Owner's choices: room code/link, +3 s per missed gate, a new tight canyon, racers pass through each other. Tested with two tabs against `test/fake-supabase.js`; the owner has since raced friends on the real service and it works.

## Guided line (built; owner's choices, asked in two interactive rounds)
Owner's words: the path was completely on the player, too hard with tricky controls; "if the player is doing right enough control, the glide is smooth and on route".
- The route is the old wind current's line (`routePoint` in game.js); in the race canyon it is bent to thread every gate's centre (`gateDh`).
- `guide(dt)`: pure pursuit to a point ~0.8 s ahead gives the tilt the line needs (`needP`, `needT`). If the player's tilt is within the level's window (and leans into any real bend or dip by at least `lean` of what it needs), `G.lock` rises in ~0.1 s (falls in ~0.25 s).
- Owner round 2: the first version replaced most of the player's steering on Easy (90%) and coasted on after a good tilt ("glides correctly for a certain distance irrespective of what we are doing"; it also felt like phone delay). Rule now: **the player's tilt always drives the glider 1:1; the line never replaces it.** Help is added on top and based only on where the glider is (lane-keeping): heading nudge `helpYaw` (yawK), height nudge `helpPitch` (pitchK), and `linePull` sideways (vertical at half). `node test/guide-response.cjs` measures how much of a quick tilt change shows while on the line: Easy turn 89% / climb 70%, Normal 93/83, Pro 98/100 (first version: Easy 11/9, Normal 40/37).
- Owner round 3: "once I move complete up then the player suddenly goes up to follow the correct path, this feels glitchy... they should join the correct path naturally". Cause: lock switched on in ~0.1 s while far off, then full help (up to 14° extra pitch) and an unlimited sideways pull yanked the glider onto the line (joined in 0.4 s, climb-rate jolt ~300 m/s²). Now: lock fades in over ~0.7 s when >4-15 m off (instant when near), the heading nudge aims 4 m along per metre off (≤ ~14°), and `linePull` is capped at `pullMax` m/s. `node test/guide-join.cjs`: joins in ~0.7 s with jolts ~80-150 (no help at all: 20-50). Trade-off: a very shaky test pilot is no longer dragged onto the line on Easy (was 87% on it, now ~20%); good pilots unchanged.
- Round 5, first review of the owner's recorded flights (ids 2-5: Mac Chrome + iPhone, direct link 5-8 ms, 60 readings/s, gyro on): gorge on Pro went well (5 m off on average); coaster on Normal went badly: a climb plus hard bend went from nothing to full tilt in ~1 s, the owner kept ~7° nose-up and no roll and was 100 m off within 3 s; while far off the line kept asking for full tilt, so steering back the right way never counted and no help came. Coaster on Pro: ~5 m off on average but only 30% 'on the line' (Pro's tilt window too strict while the position was already right). Changes: pursuit looks ~1.1 s ahead (was 0.8), and lock = max(tilt matches; on route = within 5-12 m and pointing along it, unless ignoring a real bend; steering back = far off, real tilt within ~30-60° of the needed direction, 60% strength); help reaches out to 70-140 m. Sim (gorge): rough pilot Easy 99% / Normal 93% on the line, Pro good 99%; coaster rough Easy 74%; hands-off still fails; joins and tilt response unchanged.
- Stutters: every recorded flight had 2-3 freezes of 66-217 ms in the first 4 s: the quality ladder stepping down mid-flight (each page load started at full quality). Now the steps are remembered per device (`qsteps` in localStorage), the ladder also runs on the menu, and `renderer.compile` prepares shaders at load.
- Levels (`GUIDE`): Easy window 0.5 / lean 30% / yawK 2.2 / pull 2.6 (max 8 m/s); Normal 0.3 / 45% / 1.2 / 1.4 (max 5); Pro 0.1 / 70% / 0.15 / 0.25 (`off` exists only for tests). On the line: +15% speed (`G.flow`). Default Easy. Picked under Guide on the start and pause screens.
- Race rooms: the host's level, sent in the start signal and shown in the lobby (presence carries each player's level). Gates and +3 s stay; the line leads through them.
- The path is an **energy stream** (owner's choice over neon light-rail and hologram tunnel): plasma core with pulses racing ahead, wide soft haze, two spiralling strands, sparks (`ribbonMat`, `buildRibbon`, `updateSparks`); gold-hot on the line, cool blue when drifting. It runs `STREAM_DROP` 4.5 m below the flight line: the chase camera looks along the line from ~3 m above, so a stream on the line squeezes into a sliver hidden behind the glider. It replaced the gold ribbon, which replaced the current tube; the current's own steering assists were removed (the line does that now). The HUD chip says ON THE LINE.
- Tests: `node test/guide-sim.cjs [gorge|coaster|race]` flies scripted pilots on each level (needs `npx vite --port 5173`); `node test/race-guide.cjs` checks the host's level reaches a guest (needs `FAKE_RT=1 npx vite --port 5174`). Simulated (gorge): hands-off fails on every level; Easy keeps a rough pilot on the line 87%, Normal a good one 93%, Pro a good one ~70%. Coaster is hardest (big pitch demands). Not yet played by the owner: the numbers need their feel.
